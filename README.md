# ladder-who5

[![DOI](https://zenodo.org/badge/DOI/10.5281/zenodo.23961621.svg)](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23961621) [![test](https://github.com/liaizhan/ladder-who5/actions/workflows/test.yml/badge.svg)](https://github.com/liaizhan/ladder-who5/actions/workflows/test.yml)

Open part of [108psy.com](https://108psy.com): a free AI-guided self-help
diagnostic for women in Kazakhstan (Russian, Kazakh, English).

**Author:** Aizhan Li, author of the Ladder method.

- About the author and the method: [108psy.com/ob-avtore](https://108psy.com/ob-avtore).
- Answers to common questions: [108psy.com/otvety](https://108psy.com/otvety) (in Russian).
- Psychosomatics for women: [108psy.com/psihosomatika](https://108psy.com/psihosomatika) (in Russian).

**Try it in your browser:** [liaizhan.github.io/ladder-who5](https://liaizhan.github.io/ladder-who5/demo/) (Kazakh, Russian, English; nothing is sent or stored).

This repository contains:

- `src/who5.mjs`: WHO-5 Well-Being Index scoring and bands (rule-based, no AI). MIT.
- `content/who5.{en,ru,kk}.json`: the five WHO-5 items, answer scale and result messages in three languages. CC BY 4.0.
- `content/ladder-questions.{en,ru,kk}.json`: question templates of the Ladder method (goal, number of steps, "I" step, takeaway). CC BY 4.0.
- `demo/`: the WHO-5 check as a static page, served by GitHub Pages.
- `test/`: unit tests. Run `npm test` (Node 20+).

## Use it in ChatGPT

The Ladder method is also available as a ChatGPT plugin, **Ladder by Aizhan Li**. OpenAI approved it and listed it in the ChatGPT plugin directory on 4 November 2026.

[Open Ladder by Aizhan Li in ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/plugins/plugin_asdk_app_6ada41b9737081919d0b0cd3b582b3c4)

It asks four questions: your goal, where you are now, what stands between you and the goal, and what you fear. Then it shows what holds you back. It answers in Kazakh, Russian or English, needs no sign-in, stores no answers and shows no price. The plugin contains no language model: the steps of the ladder are classified by fixed rules, the same as on the site. It does not diagnose, and on crisis wording it shows helplines instead of an analysis.

To find it, open Plugins in ChatGPT and search for Ladder.

## How it is used in the product

A woman answers the five WHO-5 items for the last two weeks. The score is computed
by `who5Score` and mapped to one of three bands by `who5Band`. The result message
for the band is fixed text, not generated by a language model. A language model
only explains the woman's Ladder in plain language within safety rules; the
wellbeing check and the crisis block do not depend on it.
